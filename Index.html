    Where the numbers come from. Station readings come from the National Environment Agency's air temperature feed on data.gov.sg (1-minute dry-bulb readings). This page checks for a new reading every minute while it is open. NEA's feed can lag, so the status line shows the time of the reading itself. The colours between stations are an inverse-distance estimate, not a measurement. The colour scale resets to fit each reading, so compare colours within one map, not between times of day.

    HDB towns. Town points come from two data.gov.sg datasets: HDB Property Information (each block's town and flat count) and HDB Existing Building (each block's footprint). Blocks were matched between them by street and block number, and each point is the flat-weighted centre of that town's residential blocks. Town temperatures are estimated from the surrounding stations; no station sits in most towns.
