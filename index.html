Stations, warmest first °C

      Where the numbers come from. Readings come live from the National Environment Agency's weather stations through data.gov.sg (1-minute dry-bulb readings). While the page is open it checks for new readings every minute. If the feed can't be reached, the page shows a saved snapshot from 30 Oct 2026, 11:54 pm instead. The colours between stations are an inverse-distance estimate, not a measurement, and the colour scale stretches to fit the current range of readings.

      MRT and LRT. Lines and stations come from sgraildata by Lim Chee Aun, built from LTA DataMall, data.gov.sg and OpenStreetMap. First and last train times and service frequencies come from LTA's station pages, collected on 11 Nov 2026. LTA doesn't publish live train arrivals online, so for real-time waiting times use the platform screens or LTA's MyTransport.SG app.

      HDB towns. Town points come from two data.gov.sg datasets: HDB Property Information (each block's town and flat count) and HDB Existing Building (each block's footprint). Blocks were matched between them by street and block number, and each point is the flat-weighted centre of that town's residential blocks. Town temperatures are estimated from the surrounding stations; no station sits in most towns.
